---
title: Icons
description: 'All the icons available in the Formwork Panel and usable in schemes and fields.'
---
Icons can be used in schemes and fields to visually represent pages, sections, or form elements. You can reference any of these icon names in the `icon` option of a page scheme or field configuration to improve clarity and user experience in the Panel.

> [!TIP]
> Click on an icon to copy its name in the clipboard.
